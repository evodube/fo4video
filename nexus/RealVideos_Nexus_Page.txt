Real Videos - Actual Video Playback on TVs, Projectors and the Drive-In


=====================================================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (Nexus summary box, under 350 characters)
=====================================================================
Fallout 4's TVs can finally play actual videos. Drop MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, MOV or WMV files into Data\Videos and watch them with sound on any TV, on Videos of the Wasteland projectors and on Rebuild's drive-in cinema. It comes with an in-game remote, playlists, directional audio, an old-film look and save-game support.

[size=5][b]What is this?[/b][/size]
For ten years every TV in the Commonwealth has looped the same static and test pattern. Video mods could only fake it with flipbook textures, animated GIF-style frame sheets or a few seconds of low-res footage baked into a mesh.

[b]Real Videos plays actual video files.[/b] Your own movies, episodes, music videos or old newsreels play in full motion at their real frame rate, with real sound coming from the screen. There are no frame sheets, no conversion to textures and no file size limits. Put a video in a folder, aim at a TV and press F6.

It is an F4SE plugin. The videos are decoded by Windows itself, either on your graphics card or on the CPU, and drawn straight onto the screen's mesh in the world.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Real video playback[/b] at the video's own frame rate, with audio, on:
[list]
[*]every vanilla TV
[*]projectors and projector screens from [b]Videos of the Wasteland[/b]
[*]the drive-in cinema screen from [b]Rebuild[/b]
[/list]
[*][b]Up to 20 screens playing at once[/b] (10 by default), each playing its own video, volume and brightness.
[*][b]In-game remote[/b] (F6) for the screen under your crosshair:
[list]
[*]play / pause, rewind and fast forward (10 s)
[*]previous / next video, home, volume up / down, mute, power
[*]a seek bar with the time, and a brightness sun (25-100%)
[*]an ALL button that turns every screen nearby on or off
[/list]
[*][b]Video search and playlists[/b] in side panels next to the remote.
[*][b]Directional sound:[/b] audio comes from the screen's direction and fades with distance. The drive-in has its own much larger hearing range.
[*][b]GPU decoding[/b] with automatic CPU fallback, plus [b]per-screen resolution caps[/b] (TVs 480p by default, projectors and cinema 1080p) to keep performance up.
[*][b]Smart pausing:[/b] screens you're not looking at, or are far from, pause their picture and sound.
[*][b]Turning off when you walk away:[/b] screens you leave behind turn off to free memory, then resume at the same spot when you come back.
[*][b]Saved with your game:[/b] screens remember their video and position across saves and loads (F4SE co-save).
[*][b]Old film look[/b] for the drive-in (on by default) and projectors (off by default), each with its own sliders:
[list]
[*]grain, scratches and dust, flicker
[*]dark corners, faded sepia colours
[*]projector shake, softness
[/list]
[*][b]Projector screens lit by the world:[/b] the frame darkens at night and brightens in daylight, while the picture itself glows.
[*][b]A full settings page[/b] in-game. Nothing needs editing by hand, although the INI is documented line by line if you prefer.
[*][b]Papyrus API[/b] for modders: Play, Stop, StopAll, IsPlaying, SetVolume and more (source included).
[/list]

[size=5][b]Supported video formats[/b][/size]
Videos are decoded by [b]Windows Media Foundation[/b], the same system the Windows Films & TV app uses. Any file Windows itself can play will work.

[b]File types:[/b] [b].mp4  .m4v  .mov  .mkv  .webm  .avi  .wmv  .asf[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Best choice: MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.[/b] It plays on every Windows install with no extra codecs and decodes fastest.
[*][b]HEVC (H.265), VP9 and AV1[/b] work if the matching free codec extension from the Microsoft Store is installed: [i]HEVC Video Extensions[/i], [i]VP9 Video Extensions[/i] and [i]AV1 Video Extension[/i].
[*]Any resolution works. Large videos are scaled down while decoding to the cap you set for each kind of screen.
[*]If a file won't play, re-encode it to H.264/AAC MP4 with HandBrake (the "Fast 1080p30" preset is fine).
[/list]

[size=5][b]How to use[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the mod and its requirements with your mod manager.
[*]Put your videos in [b]Data\Videos[/b]. In MO2 you can make a separate mod folder containing [i]Videos\[/i] so it stays in the virtual Data folder.
[*][b]Playlists:[/b] put videos in a folder like [b]Data\Videos\Playlists\Saturday Night[/b]. It shows up in the remote's Playlists panel. Next / previous stay inside that playlist. Any other subfolder also groups its videos together.
[*]In game, [b]aim at a TV, projector screen or the drive-in screen and press F6[/b] to open the remote.
[*]Pick a video from the search panel, or press play.
[*]Press [b]W/A/S/D[/b] with the remote open to walk around with it still on screen. Press Left Alt or F6 to get the cursor back, and Esc or F6 to close it.
[/list]

Every setting is in game on the remote's [b]Settings[/b] page, saved to [i]Data\F4SE\Plugins\RealVideos.ini[/i]. Distances are shown in metres.

[b]Playback[/b]
[list]
[*]Loop videos
[*]Pause in menus (Esc, Pip-Boy, loading...)

[*]Show messages (playing, paused, off)
[*]"All screens here" reaches (default 150 m)
[/list]

[*]Cinema: pause when far away or out of view (further than 150 m, for longer than 10 s)
[*]Cinema: turn off when you walk away (further than 300 m, for longer than 30 s)
[*]TVs, Projectors: pause when far away or out of view (further than 50 m, for longer than 10 s)
[*]TVs, Projectors: turn off when you walk away (further than 100 m, for longer than 30 s)
[/list]

[b]Picking a screen[/b]

[*]Use the screen under the crosshair
[*]How far you can aim (default 200 m)

[*]Cinema brightness
[*]Projectors: stretch to fill the screen
[*]Projectors: draw the video only inside the screen
[*]Two-sided screens: show on both sides
[*]Stop the screen's own animation while playing
[*]Hide glowing layers over the screen
[*]Cinema: old film look and Projectors: old film look, each with Grain, Scratches and dust, Flicker, Dark corners, Faded colours (sepia), Shake and Softness

[*]Sound comes from the screen's direction

[*]TVs & projectors: full volume up to / can't be heard past

[*]How it fades between the two

[b]Remote[/b]
[list]

[*]Lock the remote in place

[size=5][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]A video won't play / the screen stays black.[/b]
Make sure it's in Data\Videos and that Windows can play it in the Films & TV / Media Player app. If not, install the codec extension or convert it to H.264 MP4.

[b]Performance dropped with several screens running.[/b]
Lower the TV video size, keep "Decode videos on the graphics card" on, and lower "Screens playing at once". Paused and turned-off screens cost nothing.

[b]Can I hear the drive-in from far away?[/b]
Yes. Raise "Cinema: can't be heard past" on the Sound settings.

[b]Does it work with my modded TV?[/b]
Most likely. Use "Next mesh" or "Turn / flip" under Fix this screen if the picture lands in the wrong place.

[b]Where are my videos supposed to go in MO2?[/b]
Create an empty mod, add a [i]Videos[/i] folder inside it, put the files there and enable it.
